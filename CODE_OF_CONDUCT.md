# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Delta is a research laboratory. Participants should be able to disagree about algorithms, measurements, and design without personal hostility.

## Expected

- Critique ideas, measurements, and patches — not people.
- Assume good faith when a result is surprising or incremental loses.
- Credit prior work (Salsa, Adapton, Incremental, Build systems à la carte, tree-sitter, rust-analyzer).
- Do not present unmeasured claims as facts.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Deliberate misrepresentation of benchmark results
- Sustained disruption of review or discussion

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainers via the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` or the repository owner.

Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants. This document is the policy; it is not a legal contract.
